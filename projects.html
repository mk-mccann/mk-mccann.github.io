Featured · Real-time systems

Real-Time VR System for Freely Moving Rodents

A full-stack VR system processing 6-DOF motion tracking input at 360 FPS. It renders perspective-correct 3D scenes at 120 FPS with under 20 ms detection-to-projection latency, using custom shaders I wrote for real-time cube mapping and projection transformations.

Large-Scale Correlation Analysis of Neural-Behavioral Relationships

The core of my PhD. I applied linear and non-linear modeling, correlational analyses, and dimensionality reduction to understand how the mouse visual cortex integrates vision and body motion, with an automated Snakemake pipeline handling terabytes of data from preprocessing through visualization.

Computer vision

Automated Cell Segmentation & Signal Activity Extraction

A computer vision pipeline for 1-photon calcium imaging movies. It achieves accurate cell segmentation and fluorescence signal extraction with minimal user input, including demixing temporal activity from spatially overlapping cells and re-identifying cells across experimental sessions.
